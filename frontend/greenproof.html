Stewardship Principles

  • 1. Weekly Irrigation: Ensure young saplings receive 5-10 liters twice weekly during non-monsoon periods.
  • 2. Mulching: Maintain a 3-inch organic mulch ring around the base to retain soil moisture.
  • 3. Regular Inspection: Submit a monthly health check to verify ongoing survival with coordinators.
